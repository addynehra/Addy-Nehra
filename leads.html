    Recent Australian liquidity events

    A scheduled GitHub Action scans public Australian business news every day for companies that were sold, took private-equity money, listed on the ASX, were taken over, or raised capital. Each event gets a score out of 100 for how likely it is that the owners received meaningful cash. With an ANTHROPIC_API_KEY repo secret set, the top events are also researched to name the founders or owners as publicly reported. Always check the source articles before you contact anyone.
